Founder & Lead Instructor, Onyx Defense Academy, LLC

Christian Rios

Onyx Defense Academy, LLC is a USCCA Official Partner providing private concealed carry, handgun, and notary services throughout the Autryville, NC area — built around safe, practical, confidence-building instruction for responsible citizens, not just meeting the bare minimum for a permit.

Our roots trace back to founder Christian Rios, whose passion for marksmanship goes back to high school, where he competed on his JROTC rifle team — the spark that grew into a lifelong drive to help others shoot safely and with confidence.

Christian is a North Carolina Department of Justice Certified Firearms Instructor and a USCCA Certified Firearms Instructor. He previously served as a North Carolina certified Correctional Officer and completed correctional officer firearms training. He is also a certified Firefighter I & II, CPR/BLS certified, and a commissioned North Carolina Notary Public.

With roots in corrections and public safety, Onyx Defense Academy brings a real-world approach to every class. Our instruction emphasizes firearm safety, legal responsibility, defensive mindset, and practical marksmanship — not just meeting the minimum requirements to obtain a permit.

As a USCCA Official Partner, Onyx Defense Academy is committed to creating a welcoming learning environment for both new and experienced shooters. Our goal is for every student to leave with greater confidence, improved skills, and a clear understanding of how to safely and responsibly exercise their Second Amendment rights.
