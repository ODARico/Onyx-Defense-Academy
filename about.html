Christian Rios

Christian Rios is the founder and lead instructor of Onyx Defense Academy, LLC, where he is dedicated to providing safe, practical, and confidence-building firearms training for responsible citizens.

Christian is a North Carolina Department of Justice Certified Firearms Instructor, a USCCA Certified Firearms Instructor, and a commissioned North Carolina Notary Public. He is also a certified Firefighter I & II, Hazardous Materials Operations technician, CPR/BLS certified, and has extensive experience in emergency response through both the fire service and industrial emergency response.

With a background in corrections, industrial maintenance, and public safety, Christian brings a real-world approach to every class. His instruction emphasizes firearm safety, legal responsibility, defensive mindset, and practical marksmanship — not just meeting the minimum requirements to obtain a permit.

As the owner of Onyx Defense Academy, Christian is committed to creating a welcoming learning environment for both new and experienced shooters. His goal is for every student to leave with greater confidence, improved skills, and a clear understanding of how to safely and responsibly exercise their Second Amendment rights.
